Privacy Policy

What happens to your data while using ShefGuide, explained simply.

This is an MSc dissertation research prototype. Do not enter sensitive personal data in questions or uploaded handbooks.

What we store

When you register, ShefGuide stores your email address, university, home country, and programme of study. Your password is never stored as plain text — only a secure bcrypt hash.

We also save your conversation history, community Q&A posts, and personalised arrival checklist items.

Where your messages go

Your chat messages are forwarded to OpenAI (GPT-4o-mini) or Google (Gemini 3.5 Flash) depending on your selected model to generate responses. An interface badge indicates which provider is active.

Documents you attach

When you upload a PDF, its text is chunked and stored as numerical embeddings linked to your account. Only one PDF document is stored per account at a time; uploading a new file overwrites the previous one.

Using ShefGuide as a guest

Guest sessions allow you to try 5 AI questions without registering. Guest sessions automatically expire after 6 hours and are not connected to any user identity unless upgraded to an account.

Protections built in

All incoming messages pass through automated PII redaction filters (scanning for emails, phone numbers, and passport formats) before reaching LLM services.

Your control over your data

You can permanently delete any saved session from the History page, or remove your uploaded PDF document in the chat workspace at any time.

Contact & Support

For questions or feedback regarding this prototype, contact support@shefguide.edu.
