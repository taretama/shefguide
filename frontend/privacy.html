Privacy Policy

Last updated: this is a student project prototype, not a finished legal document.

ShefGuide is an academic project built to support international students navigating UK higher education. This page explains, in plain terms, what happens to your data while you use the prototype — it is not a substitute for a formal, lawyer-reviewed privacy policy, which would be required before any real-world launch.

What we store

Your email, university, home country, and programme (collected at registration), your chat messages and AI replies, any questions and replies you post to the Community Q&A board, and the personalised checklist generated for you.

Where your messages go

When you chat with ShefGuide or ask a document a question, your message is sent to either OpenAI (for GPT-4o-mini) or Google (for Gemini) to generate a response, depending on which model you select. You'll see a badge in the chat interface showing which provider is handling a given message.

Document uploads

PDFs you upload to the Document Explainer are processed to extract text, which is kept in server memory only for the duration of the session — it is not written to the database, and is lost whenever the backend restarts.

What we don't do

We don't sell your data, and we don't use it for advertising. This is a non-commercial academic prototype.

Questions

Reach out at support@shefguide.edu.
