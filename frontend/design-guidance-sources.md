# External Design-Guidance Sources

## Impeccable

Source: <https://github.com/pbakaus/impeccable>

Applied principles: avoid generic template signals, use an explicit product and design context, prioritise a structured polish pass, and keep colour, type and component choices coherent rather than adding decorative variety.

## Taste Skill

Source: <https://www.tasteskill.dev/docs>

Source repository: <https://github.com/leonxlnx/taste-skill>

Applied principles: establish a brief-led direction, protect hero discipline, keep a consistent page theme and shape system, avoid equal-weight repeated card layouts, and use a measured balance of layout variance, motion and visual density.
