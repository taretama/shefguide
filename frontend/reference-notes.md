# Home-Page Reference Findings

## Tall Learning-Platform Reference

### Tiles 1–2

The page begins with a wide, compact header inside a saturated hero field. The hero is deliberately split: oversized left-aligned statement and supporting action/statistics at left, with a contextual student image and small floating signals at right. A calm, high-contrast logo strip immediately follows to create a clear visual pause.

The next section uses a headline plus a three-step horizontal path with large numbered stops and dashed connecting rules. The course-catalogue section then changes the rhythm into a filter row and a tightly aligned visual-card grid. The transferable pattern for ShefGuide is a **split orientation hero → reassurance strip → three-stage arrival pathway → varied guide-card collection**, while retaining ShefGuide copy, map motifs and academic-support purpose.

### Adaptation Boundary

The ShefGuide design will borrow the compositional rhythm and information hierarchy, not the reference’s brand treatment, photographs, wording, pricing, ratings, course metadata or colour scheme.

### Tiles 3–4

The middle of the reference continues a dense, image-led card collection before opening into an asymmetrical proof section. The proof section pairs a large “product-in-use” visual on one side with a concise benefit list on the other, then uses a quiet interstitial statement as a full-width pause. Its later evidence section combines an editorial statement and large numeric proof with a vertical contextual photograph.

For ShefGuide, this translates into a **document-aware chat demonstration** paired with three grounded product benefits, followed by a calm full-width support-signposting statement and an editorial “why this matters” block. It does not justify fabricated metrics, testimonials, reviews or student feedback; those content types will not be created.

### Tiles 5–6

The closing structure uses one editorial preview row, then a broad, low-contrast conversion panel with a decisive action beside its statement. A dark, multi-column footer anchors the long page and reintroduces navigation only after the final action.

For ShefGuide, the adaptation will use a practical “start with your question” closing panel and a dark ink-navy footer. It will not recreate reference admissions claims, telephone numbers, subscription capture or marketing statistics.

## Second Supplied Reference

The second reference features a very wide rounded capsule navigation bar, a generously spaced two-column hero, a small category pill above a bold plain-language headline, direct paired calls to action and a singular illustrative object that carries the hero’s personality. Below, it begins a large warm-toned service panel with a left editorial explanation and two right-aligned destination cards.

The ShefGuide adaptation will take its **capsule navigation, calm left-heavy hero composition and warm destination-card framing**. The copied subject matter, black-and-beige palette, typography, illustration and service labels will not be reused.
