How do I reference a source in Harvard style?

Harvard referencing is a common author-date style used at Sheffield. The general format for a book reference in your bibliography looks like this:

Author Surname, Initial(s). (Year of publication) Title of book in italics. Edition (if not the first). Place of publication: Publisher.

For example:

Smith, J. (2023) Academic Writing for International Students. 2nd edn. London: Routledge.

In your text, you would cite it as: (Smith, 2023).
