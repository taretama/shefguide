How do I reference a YouTube video in Harvard style?

I'm using a documentary from YouTube for my essay but I'm not sure how to format it in the bibliography.

bolt Cached

AI Answer

To reference a YouTube video in Harvard style, generally use this format:

Author/Username (Year) Title of video. Available at: URL (Accessed: Date).

Example:
CrashCourse (2018) Sociology & the Scientific Method: Crash Course Sociology #3. Available at: https://www.youtube.com/... (Accessed: 12 October 2024).
